The complete wealth stack

↗
Credit Reports

AECB report history, facility reconciliation, score changes and labelled estimates. Report ingestion uses the configured agent workflow.

≡
Classify

Manage categories, payees and matching rules in one place. Review suggestions and assign spending, savings or investment buckets.

↳
Bank Agents In development

A dedicated Accounts tab for configured bank connections and sign-in prompts. Availability depends on the bank adapter and local setup.

Bank Accounts

Current accounts, savings, deposits, credit cards and loans, with balances, utilization, statement coverage and linked catalog products.

Investments & Watchlist

Multi-broker portfolio plus a live watchlist with ADX, DFM, US and crypto quotes — card, fit and list views with drag-to-reorder.

Real Estate

Leases, tenants, rent schedules, mortgages and expenses — plus full off-plan support with payment-plan milestones and co-ownership shares.

Vehicles & Valuables

Cars, boats, watches, jewellery — tracked with purchase price, current value, documents (Mulkiya, insurance) and value-vs-purchase at a glance.

Transactions

Statement import with duplicate detection, payee normalization rules, automatic recurring-payment detection, and a clean review queue.

Pots, Goals & Budget

Envelope budgeting with sinking funds, computed monthly set-asides from real due dates, and a planner that refuses to double-count.

Cheques

Track written, cleared, bounced and cancelled cheques from an account’s Chequebook, with due dates and links to account transactions.

Product Catalog & Alerts

Products, comparisons, offers, rate alerts and KFS history. Refreshes come from the optional Intel service; coverage and freshness vary by source.

Hosted by you

Store financial records and documents on your own hardware. Online quotes, catalog refreshes and optional integrations connect to external services when used.

How it works

From records to a clearer picture

01
Add what you own

Create your accounts, brokers, properties and tangibles. Set currencies, ownership shares and opening balances.

02
Import your statements

Import a supported PDF or CSV format. Review the detected account, transaction rows, duplicate flags and reconciliation before confirming.

03
See your full picture

Follow net worth, portfolio performance, credit history and property equity. Classify spending and turn upcoming commitments into a plan.
